Our Story

A family grill, right in the heart of Kampala.

Kampala Flame Grill started as a single charcoal stove and a simple idea: cook a short menu extremely well. Every piece of chicken is marinated overnight and grilled to order, and every order comes with our free house sides — creamy mayo and a fresh-blended tomato, green chilli and garlic sauce.
